# FocusTones Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Publisher: Tyler Cyert, an individual developer

Privacy and support contact: [sudo@tyl.sh](mailto:sudo@tyl.sh)

FocusTones makes sound on your iPhone. You do not need a FocusTones account to listen.

## Information on your device

FocusTones keeps your saved mixes, last mix, settings, session records and focus time on your device. A session record includes when a session started, how long it lasted, its mix name and band, and the time zone used for its day. The app shares limited current listening information with its widget and other iPhone controls on the same device. FocusTones does not operate a service that uploads or synchronizes your listening history.

Backups to iCloud or a computer may include this local app data, depending on your backup settings. Resetting or removing FocusTones does not erase backup copies already made; those copies follow your backup arrangements.

If you enable a daily reminder or a trial reminder, FocusTones asks iOS for notification permission and schedules it on your device. Listening does not require notification permission. You can change permission in iPhone Settings.

## Purchases and Apple diagnostics

Apple processes Pro purchases, billing and restoration through the App Store. FocusTones uses Apple's StoreKit to load products and check verified purchase status so it can enable Pro. This direct StoreKit version does not configure RevenueCat or send purchase information to a FocusTones server. Tyler Cyert does not receive payment card details through the app. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Apple may make purchase and sales reports available to Tyler Cyert through App Store Connect. Tyler may view or download those reports to understand sales, handle accounting and answer purchase questions. Apple controls its own purchase records; a report Tyler downloads is a separate copy he controls.

FocusTones includes no advertising, behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Apple may collect crash and diagnostic information under its practices. TestFlight crash reports are automatically shared with developers, even when a tester has not enabled device diagnostics sharing. Tyler Cyert does not access FocusTones crash or diagnostic reports in Xcode Organizer.

## Retention, deletion and requests

Your local mixes, settings, session records and focus time remain on your device until you use Reset All Data in FocusTones Settings or remove the app. Reset also removes the app's shared widget information and scheduled reminders. These actions do not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple's purchase records or existing iCloud or computer backups. Apple handles requests about its own purchase, account and iCloud backup records; computer backups remain under your control.

Tyler Cyert keeps support correspondence and any reports he downloads only while needed to answer requests, handle accounting or maintain the site. He does not promise a fixed deletion date for those records. Service providers hold their own records under their policies and settings. To ask what information Tyler holds, request access or deletion, or ask about retention, email [sudo@tyl.sh](mailto:sudo@tyl.sh). Tyler will review the request and explain what he can delete and what is held by Apple or another provider.

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
