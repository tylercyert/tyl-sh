# FocusTones Support

Questions about FocusTones, a problem with the app, or help with Pro? Email [sudo@tyl.sh](mailto:sudo@tyl.sh).

If you are reporting a problem, tell us what happened and, if you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the FocusTones version shown at the bottom of Settings. Do not send your Apple Account password or payment card details.

## Restore Pro

In FocusTones, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. If Pro still does not appear, email [sudo@tyl.sh](mailto:sudo@tyl.sh).
